BreakoutAI

A daily stock market data platform for the US and India.

4

guards against bad data

A bad run fails red and yesterday's data keeps serving

  1. fetch

    Login failed. Most prices came back blank.

    Guard 1. Retry the login once.
    Guard 2. Date each record to the bar it actually priced.

  2. checks

    Blanks got through. NaN passes a "<= 0" check.

    Guard 3. Reject any price that is not a finite number.

  3. publish

    Published anyway.

    Guard 4. Abort the publish if the stock count falls below 67% of yesterday's.

  4. site

    64% of India stocks had no price. The site went down.

    The run fails red. Yesterday's good data keeps serving.

What I'd tell you about it. The login retry was not the real fix. The failure was intermittent, so the downstream guards are what keep bad data off the site.

How I work

I find out why two numbers don't match, then build the pipeline so they always do.

  1. 01

    Reconcile before you report.

    Every number I publish has a second source it must tie to.

    • Reconciliation agents
    • Mismatch alerts
    • Audited reports
  2. 02

    Fail loudly, keep serving.

    A broken run should stop and leave yesterday's good data live, not publish quietly.

    • BreakoutAI guards
  3. 03

    Replace the spreadsheet, keep the logic auditable.

    Automation that finance can trace line by line.

    • 25 workbooks to one refresh
  4. 04

    Make it faster where people wait.

    Tune the step someone sits and watches.

    • 3 h to 30 min
    • 90 to 10 min

    Leave the team stronger.

    Review the code, train the people who use the numbers, and help hire the next engineers.
